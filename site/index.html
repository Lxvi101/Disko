Tonight's lineup

Everyone who's been
hogging the floor.

Headliner

Xcode, live on the main stage.

Simulators you forgot about, device support for every iOS you ever plugged in, DerivedData from projects you deleted two years ago. Pick what stays, one by one.

Optional

Ask what's safe to drop.

With the Codex CLI installed, the assistant reads your scan and explains what's worth removing. It's read-only and can't delete a thing.

Junk that grows back anyway.

Caches and build output that get rebuilt on demand, each with a reason. Tools with their own cleanup command, like npm, Homebrew and uv, point you to it instead.

Guests who never left.

Leftovers from apps you uninstalled, and the old versions Adobe, Cursor and Claude Code leave behind.

The wallflowers.

Big files you haven't opened in months, sorted by size.

Coat check.

Nothing is deleted straight away. It waits in quarantine until you're sure.
